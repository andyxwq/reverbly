Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Reverbly (“we”) provides a browser-based audio tool. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we process

  • Account email — for passwordless sign-in and subscription notices.
  • Plan and export metadata — preset settings, timestamps, and quota counters so free limits and Pro features work.
  • Optional export files — WAV files you choose to keep in export history are stored in a private bucket tied to your account.
  • Payment data — handled by Stripe. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Anonymous export id — a random id in your browser local storage to enforce the free daily export without an account.

Audio in the browser

Editing runs locally in your browser with the Web Audio API. We do not upload your original audio unless you export while signed in and storage succeeds for history.

Processors

We use Supabase (auth, database, storage) and Stripe (billing). Their privacy terms apply to data they process on our behalf.

Retention

Account data stays while your account exists. Export history files may be removed after a retention period or when you delete your account. Contact us to request deletion.
