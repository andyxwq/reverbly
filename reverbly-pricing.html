Questions

Short answers before you subscribe.

What is the Pro trial?

7 days of Pro for free. Stripe shows the charge date before you confirm. Cancel before the trial ends and you are not charged.

Plus vs Pro?

Plus is unlimited exports and history at $3.99. Pro adds saved presets and higher file limits at $7.99.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Manage or cancel from your account via the Stripe customer portal.

Do I need an account for Free?

No for your first exports. Create an account when you want Plus, Pro, or history on another device.
